For your buyers

Ordering takes under a minute

Made for phones

Big buttons, a running total pinned to the bottom of the screen. No app to download, just a link or a QR code.

Build the order

Pick one or more mains (turkey + pastrami in one panini), add extras, choose 1 to 10. The price adds up as they tap.

Delivery or pickup

Delivery to their room for a small fee you set (default $1), or free pickup.

Special requests

An "Anything special?" note for things like "no mustard" or "extra cheese".

Pay by Zelle or cash

Zelle: the site shows your number and the exact amount. Cash: they pay when they get their food. You can turn cash off anytime. No card fees.

"Sold out" when you are

Close the shop and the site says Sold out right away. Nobody can order, or pay, after you close.

For you, the seller

Run it all from your phone

All your orders in one list

Name, room, what they ordered, any note, and the time. New orders show up by themselves every 15 seconds.

Paid ✓ and Done ✓

Each order says Cash or Zelle. Tick Paid once you have the money. Tick Done when the food is handed over; the order fades out but stays listed.

New

Order alerts

Get a Telegram message or an email the moment someone orders: who, what, which room, how much.

New

Analytics

Orders today and this week, sales, average order, your busiest hour, and your best sellers.

New

Maaser calculator

10% of your paid sales, this month and all time, worked out for you automatically.

Sold out, item by item

Out of crepes? Mark just that item sold out. Or close the whole shop with one button and open it again tomorrow.

New

Edit your menu from the admin page

Change names and prices, add new items and extras, or remove one. Buyers see it the moment you save.

New

Discounts

Tap Discount next to any item and type the sale price. Buyers see the old price crossed out and a Sale sticker.

New

Photos and descriptions

Add a photo and a short description to any item from the admin page, or remove them. Buyers see a small photo on the item, so the menu stays clean.

Your data, your Sheet

Every order and your menu are saved to your own Google Sheet, so you always have a full record.

Prices can't be faked

Totals are checked against your menu on the server, so nobody can change the price in their browser.

Your own look

Your shop name, colors, picture and words. Your own link, plus a printable QR code.

Getting started

Live in about a day

  1. Tell us your menu

    Your items and prices, your Zelle number, and whether you deliver.

  2. We build your shop

    Your own page at mikdashmunchies.github.io/your-shop, plus a QR code to print or post.

  3. Share the link and start selling

    Orders show up on your phone. You tick them off as you go.
